## Shared Colloquial Controls

| Concept | Meaning | Example phrases |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `NATURAL` | Reduce styling; use everyday actions and a small imperfection. | 更真实; more natural; より自然に; 더 자연스럽게; более естественно; plus naturel; más natural |
| `RAW_CANDID` | Use bolder framing, foreground occlusion, less eye contact, and slight movement. | 更野一点; make it more raw; もっとラフに; 좀 더 날것처럼; более сыро; plus brut; más crudo |
| `PHONE` | Use portable-device perspective and automatic-exposure character. | 手机感; phone snapshot; スマホ写真風; 휴대폰 스냅; как на телефон; style smartphone; como foto de móvil |
| `FILM` | Add restrained grain and gentle highlight behavior. | 胶片感; film look; フィルム風; 필름 느낌; плёночный вид; look pellicule; estilo película |
| `TELEPHOTO_OBSERVER` | Use a distant observer position, telephoto compression, and plausible occlusion for a fictional, consent-based visual style. | 狗仔感; paparazzi style; パパラッチ風; 파파라치 스타일; папарацци; style paparazzi; estilo paparazzi |

When a phrase is ambiguous, preserve the literal user constraints and choose the least assumptive shared concept.
